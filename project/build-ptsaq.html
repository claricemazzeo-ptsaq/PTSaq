3 · Repositório de documentos

O módulo espelha a pasta mestra /folders/1rtRecy4IjFejSXOG7UHsVEYm-4h7QoBM em modo somente leitura. O app nunca renomeia, move ou exclui arquivo; o que ele grava fica nas duas planilhas.

Cartão de documento

Ladrilho de extensão 42px (a cor vem do tipo de arquivo, não do departamento) · título em duas linhas no máximo · versão, data e tamanho numa linha só · badge de situação · contador de vínculos · marca de fixado offline.

Vínculo cruzado

O vínculo é bidirecional e vive no documento: a tarefa lista os arquivos, o arquivo lista tarefas e metas. Assim Adesivação de salas chega ao Cerberus Brandbook em um toque, e vice-versa.

Sincronização de arquivos

Polling em segundo plano lê apenas metadados (nome, versão, data, proprietário) — barato e rápido. O binário só é baixado quando o usuário abre a prévia ou fixa offline. “Buscar documentos novos” força a leitura.

Offline

Fixar guarda o arquivo no aparelho e o mantém atualizado enquanto houver rede. Sem rede, os fixados abrem normalmente; os demais aparecem esmaecidos com a data da última versão conhecida.

4 · Padrões de sincronização

Leitura

Automática, com puxar para atualizar

Polling a cada 60s em primeiro plano. O gesto de puxar existe como confirmação, não como obrigação: o usuário puxa quando quer certeza, não porque o app depende disso. A pílula no topo mostra sempre o horário do último dado confirmado, nunca “agora”.

Escrita

Otimista, com fila visível

A tela reflete a edição imediatamente e a operação entra numa fila com estado próprio (na fila · enviando · gravado). Só as colunas Status e Observação são graváveis; fórmulas e colunas calculadas nunca são tocadas.

Conflito

Escolha explícita, nunca silenciosa

Quando a mesma célula muda dos dois lados, o app não decide. Mostra as duas versões com autor e horário e pede uma escolha. A versão descartada vira comentário na célula, então nada se perde.

Offline

Leitura completa, escrita enfileirada

Sem rede, tudo continua legível a partir do cache e a pílula fica âmbar com o horário do último dado. As edições ficam na fila e sobem sozinhas ao reconectar; o contador na barra inferior diz quantas.

5 · O que os dados não trazem

Três lacunas nas planilhas afetam diretamente o que a interface consegue mostrar. Nenhuma foi preenchida com número inventado.

  • Valor orçado: só a marcenaria da recepção (R$ 38.000,00) tem valor válido. As demais células da coluna F contêm números de linha, não moeda. O KPI de orçamento mostra a cobertura — 1 de 24 — junto do total.
  • Percentual da meta: a coluna G do Plano de Trabalho também traz sequências de linha (37, 42, 47, 51…), não progresso. As barras derivam da Situação: Concluída 100%, Em andamento 50%, A iniciar 0%.
  • Numeração das metas: existem 3.1.2 e 3.1.3 sem 3.1.1, e a meta 3.1 tem cronograma “Mês 24”. Reproduzido como está, com aviso na tela de detalhe.

Sections 6–9 are written in English, as requested. Product copy inside the app stays in pt-BR, matching the team that uses it.

6 · Real-time bi-directional engine

Two channels, one direction each. Reads arrive over a persistent stream; writes leave over plain HTTPS and are confirmed by the same stream, so a change never appears twice.

READ PATH  (Sheets/Drive → device, target latency < 2 s)

  Google Sheets edit ─┐
  Drive folder change ┤→ Drive push webhook → sync service
                       → diff against last known revision
                       → SSE frame to every subscribed device
                       → local cache write → UI re-render

WRITE PATH  (device → Sheets, target confirmation < 3 s)

  status / note edit → optimistic UI + local queue entry
                     → POST /cells  {sheet, row, column, value,
                                     baseRevision, actor}
                     → guard: baseRevision matches?
                          yes → batchUpdate on the three writable
                                columns only, then SSE echo → "gravado"
                          no  → 409 → conflict sheet opens on device

Transport

Server-Sent Events, not WebSockets: the read path is one-way, SSE reconnects on its own, and it survives mobile network changes without a heartbeat protocol. The status bar shows Sincronização ao vivo · SSE conectado only while the stream is open — never as a decorative label.

Concurrency

Presence is published per item, so opening a task announces it. When someone else holds the item — Rhanon in Sheets, Ana in the app — the detail sheet shows a live presence line before the edit controls, which is warning enough to prevent most collisions. The ones that get through are caught by the revision guard.

Formula protection

The write endpoint accepts three column keys and nothing else: Status, Observações, Percentual da meta. Values go in as RAW, never USER_ENTERED, so a note beginning with “=” lands as text. Rows are addressed by a stable key column, not by index, so a sort in Sheets cannot misdirect a queued write.

Failure behaviour

A dropped stream degrades to 60-second polling and the bar turns amber; it never silently pretends to be live. Writes retry with exponential backoff and stay in the visible queue until the server echoes them back.

Cache

Both sheets in full plus document metadata — roughly 1 MB. Pinned files add their own weight and are listed with their size before pinning, so nobody fills a phone by accident.

Queue

Edits queue in order with three visible states — na fila · enviando · gravado. Two edits to the same cell collapse into the last one. On reconnect the queue drains oldest first; anything that fails the revision guard surfaces as a conflict rather than being dropped.

Honest staleness

Offline, every screen keeps the timestamp of the data it is showing. The app never presents cached numbers as current — the cost of a wrong budget figure in a meeting is higher than the cost of an extra line of text.

10 · Authentication and onboarding

Google Workspace sign-in is the primary path — the data already lives in Workspace, so a second credential store adds risk without adding access. Email and password exist as the fallback for staff without a corporate account yet, and both paths land in the same approval gate.

Splash
└── Login
    ├── Google corporate sign-in (OAuth 2.0, hosted domain pinned)
    │   ├── domain matches  → session → Dashboard
    │   └── domain unknown  → Pending approval
    ├── Email + password (visibility toggle, 10-char minimum)
    │   └── "Esqueci minha senha" → reset link to corporate inbox
    └── Create account
        ├── full name · corporate email · password · front
        └── submit → Pending approval
                     └── executive approves in-app → Dashboard

Live connection chip is present on the login screen itself: the app
states whether the server and Workspace are reachable BEFORE the user
spends a credential on a request that cannot succeed.

Pending accounts see no operational data at all — not a greyed-out shell, not a demo dataset. The approval queue lives on the executive's own production screen rather than in a separate admin console, because approving two accounts a month does not justify a second surface.

11 · Personal production dashboard

This is the home screen — first tab, and where every session lands after login. It answers one question, what is mine today and what is in the way, filtered to rows where Responsável matches the signed-in person across both spreadsheets. The macro view moved to the third slot: a park-wide KPI is something a lead consults, not something they open the app for.

Dependencies are no longer a separate section. Each queue card carries its own state — an amber banner naming who is blocking it, or a coral one saying whose work it will unlock — so the person reads cause and action in the same glance instead of reconciling two lists. Sort order follows that: cards that block a colleague first, then blocked cards, then by deadline.

Profile header

Avatar, name, job title, front tag, and an availability control with three states — No escritório · Em campo / obra · Modo foco. Availability is published on the same presence channel as item presence, so a colleague sees it before pinging.

Counters

Assigned · executed · pending · held. “Held” counts items with an unresolved dependency owned by someone else — the only number on the screen that another person can lower.

Active queue

One primary action per card, computed from the current status: Iniciar for a not-started item, Marcar executado for one in progress. A secondary Anotar opens the detail sheet with the note field. Rhanon, on a scaffold with one hand free, changes a status in one tap.

Two dependency lists

“Esperando outras pessoas” in amber, “Pessoas esperando por você” in coral. The second list is deliberately the louder of the two: a person's own delay is visible to them before anyone has to ask.

Below the lists, the documents linked to that person's own items — Rhanon's carpentry drawings, Clarice's brandbook, Ana's contract drafts — then a personal activity log mirroring what was written back to Sheets. The session switcher carries seven people, including the two contributors, so the same layout can be checked against very different row sets.

  ├─ t+<1 s    SSE frame + push to Clarice's device
  └─ on Clarice's screen
       ├─ badge Bloqueada (amber) → Liberada (green), 4 pulses
       ├─ toast "Ana Flávia concluiu 'Termo de cessão de uso'.
       │         A tarefa 'Adesivação de salas' está liberada."
       ├─ card moves out of "Esperando outras pessoas"
       └─ "held" counter drops by one

If Clarice is offline
  the event waits in her inbox; the badge transition plays on the
  next foreground, so she never misses the unlock — she just sees
  it late, with the original timestamp attached.

The pulse runs four times and stops. An indefinite animation would still be moving hours later, when it means nothing.

13 · Data isolation by role

Three scopes, one dataset. Personal filters to rows where the person is Responsável and is the only scope with one-tap write actions. Department gives full read and write inside the person's own front. Cross-departmental is read-only with a ping action — visibility is never restricted, because a park where four fronts cannot see each other is the problem this app exists to fix. The executive holds all three plus account approval and conflict resolution. Filtering happens server-side; the device never receives rows it is not entitled to write, so an intercepted cache cannot be edited into the spreadsheet.
