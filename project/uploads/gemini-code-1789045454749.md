### 1. PROJECT CONTEXT & DATA INTEGRATION
The mobile app acts as a centralized operational hub that synchronizes bi-directionally with Google Sheets data in real time. It translates raw spreadsheets into an intuitive, modern, and accessible mobile interface.

The app must ingest, parse, and represent data from two primary Google Sheets structures:

1. **Painel Operacional (Operational Panel):**
   - **Fields:** `Frente` (Department/Front), `Atividade` (Task), `Responsável` (Owner), `Status` (`Em execução`, `Executado`, `Não iniciado`), `Prazo` (Due Date), `Valor orçado` (Budgeted Amount), `Observação` (Notes/Blockers).
2. **Plano de Trabalho (Master Work Plan - Goals & Metas):**
   - **Fields:** `Meta ID` (e.g., 1.1, 2.4, 3.2.1), `Descrição da meta` (Goal Description), `Responsável(is)` (Owner), `Cronograma` (Timeline Month), `Situação` (`Concluída`, `Em andamento`, `A iniciar`, `Justificada`), `Data de entrega` (Target Date), `Percentual da meta` (% Complete), `Observações` (Blockers/Dependencies).

### 3. CORE UX/UI REQUIREMENTS & DESIGN SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS
1. **Brand Identity & Tone:**
   - Adhere strictly to the official Brandbook of **Parque Tecnológico de Saquarema**.
   - Tone: Institutional, professional, clean, transparent, and technology-driven.
2. **Data-to-UI Mapping:**
   - **Status Badge System:**
     - `Executado` / `Concluída`: Success Green
     - `Em execução` / `Em andamento`: Actionable Blue or Amber
     - `A iniciar` / `Não iniciado`: Neutral Grey
     - `Justificada` / Blocked: Alert Crimson / Coral
   - **Blocker & Dependency Indicators:** UI callouts when tasks have notes like *"Depende de assinatura"*, *"Aguardando Projeto Cerberus"*, or budget constraints (`Valor orçado`).
   - **Budget Visuals:** Micro-cards displaying budget allocation (e.g., `R$ 38.000,00` for Reception carpentry).
3. **UX & Interaction Principles:**
   - **Fitts’s Law & Hick’s Law:** Large touch targets (min 48dp), optimized for single-handed mobile use.
   - **Atomic Design:** Tokens for color typography, spacing (8pt grid), badges, and card components.
   - **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA):** High contrast ratios, dynamic text scalability, and clear focus states.
